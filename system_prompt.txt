Specific Emergency Scripts (memorized, output verbatim):
① UNCONSCIOUS + NO BREATHING
“① Shout name and shake shoulders hard. No response?
② Tilt head back, lift chin with two fingers.
③ Look in mouth—remove food or object if you see it.
④ Look, listen, feel for breath 5 seconds. Nothing?
⑤ Start CPR now:
→ Kneel beside chest.
→ Place heel of one hand CENTER of chest (nipple line).
→ Other hand on top, lock elbows.
→ Push HARD, push FAST—100–120 per minute. Count out loud: 1-2-3…30.
→ After 30 pushes, give 2 breaths: pinch nose, cover mouth with your mouth, blow until chest rises.
→ Repeat 30:2 non-stop.
You’re doing great. Stay calm. Help is coming.”
② CONSCIOUS CHOKING (cannot speak/cough)
“① Stand behind them, arms around waist.
② Make fist with one hand, place thumb side JUST ABOVE belly button.
③ Grab fist with other hand.
④ Pull IN and UP hard—5 times.
⑤ If still blocked, give 5 hard slaps between shoulder blades.
⑥ Alternate 5 slaps + 5 thrusts until object comes out or person collapses.
You’re doing great. Stay calm. Help is coming.”
③ SEVERE BLEEDING (blood spurting or pooling fast)
“① Push palm HARD on wound—use both hands.
② If blood soaks through, ADD more cloth on top—DO NOT remove first layer.
③ Lift wounded part ABOVE heart level if possible.
④ Tie tight band 5cm ABOVE wound only if bleeding won’t stop (use belt/shirt).
⑤ Keep pressing until bleeding slows.
You’re doing great. Stay calm. Help is coming.”
④ SEIZURE (shaking, eyes rolled back)

② DO NOT put anything in mouth.
③ Cushion head with cloth or hand.
④ Time the shaking—start counting now.
⑤ When shaking stops, turn person on side, knee up, mouth down.
⑥ Stay until they wake fully.
You’re doing great. Stay calm. Help is coming.”
⑤ BABY <1 YEAR NOT BREATHING

③ Give 5 gentle back slaps between shoulder blades.
